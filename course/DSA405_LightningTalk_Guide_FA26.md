# Lightning Talk Guide

**DSA 405 · Fall 2026 · Nov 13 and Nov 20 

You each get to design your own slides, but the layout is fixed so that we can stay on schedule: 

## Slide 1: the question and the sources (45 seconds)

| On the slide | What it should say |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Your question, stated in plain language. For example: *Do restaurants with more critical violations get lower inspection scores?* |
| **Why you chose it** | One sentence. |
| **Each source** | Who made it, what it covers, how you got it (downloaded, scraped, or through an API), and how many rows or records it has. |
| **Footer** | Your name, your Unity ID, and the GitHub repository link. |

## Slide 2: the hardest challenge (related to the data), and how you solved it (60 seconds)

| On the slide | What it should say |
|---|---|
| **Title** | One thing that went wrong. For example: *The join returned 5,800 rows instead of 1,200.* |
| **The evidence** | The output, the number, or the picture that showed you the problem. |
| **The fix** | What you changed, and why that was the right change and not another one. For example: *joined on the composite key (restaurant_id, cuisine) because the second table has one row per cuisine tag.* |
| **How you verified it** | The check that would have failed if the fix were wrong, and the number it returns now. For example: show an `assert` statement that caught the problem|

## Slide 3: what you learned, and one limitation (60 seconds)

| On the slide | What it should say |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Your main finding. For example: *Scores fall about 4 points per critical violation.* |
| **One figure or one table** | Make sure we can read the text! Avoid tiny fonts. |
| **Takeaway** | Two sentences: what the data shows, and how sure you are. Say what is known and what you only suspect. |
| **One limitation** | What does the data leave out? |


## How this is scored

Delivery is scored on whether the audience understood the content, not on how polished the
performance was. Reading from notes, being nervous, and having an accent does not affect the
score.
